How it works

No new software for your team. We plug into the browser session your front-desk already has open.

  1. 01

    2-hour onboarding

    One Zoom with your office manager. We walk through your PIMS — ezyVet, Cornerstone, AVImark, or Provet Cloud — map where waitlist and cancellations live, and approve your message templates. You sign a one-page CASL consent record.

  2. 02

    Automation runs in your session

    A small headless browser, authenticated as a least-privilege staff user your team creates, watches the schedule. When a slot opens during business hours, it pulls the recall + waitlist queue, ranks by recency and proximity, and drafts outreach.

  3. 03

    Front-desk approves with one tap

    Outbound messages land in a Slack or SMS channel for your team. One thumbs-up sends. Auto-send is opt-in for clinics that want it after week 4.

  4. 04

    You see the dollars

    Monthly PDF report — slots opened, slots refilled, dollars rescued, response-time percentiles. No vanity metrics. If we don't beat the retainer 3×, we refund.
